# The project

Allium starts with a modest question: can an onion-price number be traced back to the same place, the same onions, and the same bag?

The work is onion-price data infrastructure: preserving public reports, making comparable observations easier to inspect, and publishing calculations that another reader can reproduce. The first proposed series is [one regional shipping-point reference](benchmark.md), not a national average and not a continuous stream of transactions.

**Allium Gazette** tells the community-facing story. **$ONION** is the associated memecoin. Buying an onion farm remains the founder's dirt-covered goal. A possible physical-onion perpetual is a later, separately conditional undertaking.

## Four strands, four different records

| Strand | Proposed role | Current boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Data infrastructure | History, comparisons, exports, reports, integrations, and an eventual documented API | Source feasibility and automated ingestion are not yet established |
| $ONION | Associated memecoin | Original source is available; no verified deployment or launch is claimed |
| Farm | A purchase by a future founder-owned LLC | No LLC, land acquisition, acreage, or secured first season is claimed |
| ONION-PERP | Possible market referencing independently defined physical onion prices | No live oracle, venue, secured partners, or launch commitment |

These strands can have real economic connections. If founder token sales or actual creator fees help finance a farm, that should be disclosed—not hidden behind a claim that the founder's finances can never intersect with token activity. Those connections do not give token holders ownership or income rights.

## A useful data business

USDA makes [Specialty Crops Market News information available at no cost](https://www.ams.usda.gov/market-news/fruits-vegetables). Allium's proposed commercial value is normalization, provenance, reliability, and workflow—not pretending to own government reports.

Potential services include paid exports, custom reports, integrations, and eventually subscriptions or benchmark licensing when demand is established. Paid pilots would help determine whether customers find the records useful before an unproven national benchmark is sold. Operating resources and customer demand are not secured, and no budget or committed financing is invented here.

Data-service income belongs to its operator. Holding $ONION does not provide company revenue or paid-data entitlement. Market capitalization and trading volume are not operating cash.

## What would count as progress?

A defensible first series would have demonstrated source access, a precisely documented product filter, measured historical coverage, preserved evidence, reproducible calculations, and explicit freshness and revision handling. Farm progress would require an actual purchase or first-season agreement and safe, permitted records. Any token launch would require the [outstanding public disclosures](token.md). A market would require the independent [legal, oracle, security, and capital gates](market.md).

Plans may change, be delayed, or stop. A useful data service could exist without a farm purchase or derivatives market; none of those outcomes guarantees a token price or return.
