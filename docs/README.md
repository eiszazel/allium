# Allium — the reference desk

Better onion numbers. Eventually, actual onions.

Allium is an onion-price data-infrastructure project. **Allium Gazette** is its community-facing publication; **$ONION** is its associated memecoin. Buying an onion farm is a founder goal, not something token holders are financing or acquiring together.

This reference desk separates what the source says, what has happened, and what remains proposed. You can read it without a wallet.

## The current record

The original [Bulb.sol source](https://github.com/eiszazel/allium/blob/main/contracts/Bulb.sol) specifies a fixed-supply ERC-20. Source availability and local inspection are not evidence of a deployed or explorer-verified token, and are not a security audit. No verified deployment address or purchase route is presented here.

The price methodology and automated data service are proposed; no Allium feed or oracle is live. No farm purchase, LLC formation, secured season, or published farm record set is claimed. The perpetual market is conditional, not operating. Reporting schedules are plans, not completed reports.

Holding $ONION confers **no land, equity, crop, business revenue, paid-data access, redemption rights, management rights, or guaranteed returns**. This publication is not a crowdfunding offer, a trading venue, or legal approval. There is no promised acquisition or market-launch date.

These Markdown pages are the public reference edition, separate from internal implementation plans. They also form the source for GitBook-compatible publishing; an authorized remote GitBook publication is not claimed.

Source and proposal review date: **October 3, 2026**. External requirements and mutable report links can change; dated examples are not live quotations.
