# Risks and historical record

The joke is an onion. The risks are real.

Allium publishes a proposed project and the original token source, not a live token offering, an operating derivatives venue, or legal approval. No funds are accepted by this reference publication. No USDA or Hyperliquid endorsement is implied.

## The historical record

The Onion Futures Act was enacted **August 28, 1958**. The present [7 U.S.C. §13-1](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/7/13-1) prohibits contracts for future delivery in onions on or subject to the rules of a board of trade in the United States. Onion prices did not stop being useful: [USDA Market News](https://www.ams.usda.gov/market-news) continues to publish agricultural information.

The CFTC's July 13, 2011 agricultural-commodity-definition final rule includes the historical statement **“Onion swaps would seem to be permissible.”** The wording is in the [published rule](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2011-07-13/html/2011-17626.htm). It is not a project-specific opinion, exemption, safe harbor, or permission to distribute $ONION or operate ONION-PERP.

A perpetual requires analysis of its actual instrument, operator, venue, participants, jurisdictions, and promotion. Calling something a swap, using an offshore entity, restricting access, or operating a testnet is not a substitute for legal review. The Gazette's historical headline is not a claim that every onion-related instrument is either prohibited or authorized.

## Token and founder risks

A memecoin can lose value sharply, lack liquidity, or become worthless. Progress on data or a farm does not promise token appreciation. Original source, compilation, and local behavior checks are not an audit, production deployment, or evidence of safe operational custody.

Actual distribution, founder holdings and wallets, fees, compensation, sales, vesting, and liquidity ownership/withdrawal controls remain material [pending disclosures](token.md). Fixed source supply alone does not answer who controls tokens or liquidity. Founder sales and fees can create conflicts; disclosure is necessary but does not eliminate risk.

Holding $ONION grants no land, equity, crops, farm or company revenue, paid-data access, redemption, management rights, or guaranteed return. A memecoin label and absence of those rights do not settle legal classification.

## Farm and reporting risks

No land, future LLC, first season, partner, or acquisition date is secured. Plans can fail, be delayed, change, or stop. Weather, pests, crop failures, costs, and unsuccessful commercial arrangements can produce losses. There is no promised harvest or community gift.

The [reporting cadence](field-notes.md) is planned, not delivered. Redactions protect people but limit outside verification. Hashes can check the integrity of identified published bytes; they do not authenticate documents, prove title, establish completeness, or verify the underlying events. Farm records are not benchmark observations and must not determine the independent benchmark price.

## Data risks

A regional quote is not a national price. Reported ranges and a calculated midpoint are not a transaction-weighted average. Shipping-point and terminal-market prices differ. Reporting can be periodic, missing, stale, revised, concentrated, or seasonal. Historical PDF availability does not establish complete structured coverage, and documented API access has not yet been exercised by this project.

A public report is not automatically suitable for derivatives. Normalization errors, duplicate observations, source outages, inconsistent units, and undisclosed methodology changes can mislead readers. The [proposed pipeline](data-pipeline.md) describes safeguards, not proven reliability or uptime.

## Market and capital risks

No oracle, perpetual, stake provider, market maker, venue partner, operating budget, or legal route is secured. HIP-3 requirements can change. Stake can be slashed, including through incompetent operation or compromised keys; stake is not order-book liquidity. A quoted protocol requirement is not a project financing commitment.

A data series may never qualify as a defensible oracle. Even if it does, legal, security, operating, or liquidity requirements may prevent launch. The [market conditions](market.md) are separate gates, not an announced timeline.

## Read facts and plans differently

A published source says what that source contains. A dated observation says what was inspected at that time. A proposed policy says what is intended, not what has been delivered. Remote publication, source availability, local inspection, and deployed contract verification are different claims.

This public reference supersedes earlier illustrative allocations and token-funded-venture framing. Allium is data infrastructure; $ONION is the associated memecoin; a founder-funded farm remains a goal. There is no public raise, funding meter, holder yield, or guaranteed return.
