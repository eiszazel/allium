# Field Notes

The community gets the record, not the real estate. Field Notes is the proposed public account of a future farm season; **there are no completed farm reports or published farm record hashes claimed here**.

## Planned cadence

Once a season is underway, the intention is **one public post each week** and **one hash of the published record set each month**, with Arc contemplated for the hash publication. No reporting or on-chain publication mechanism is represented as live.

The account could cover planting, costs, irrigation failures, setbacks, harvests, onions sold, and money lost. A failed season belongs in the record as much as a successful one. Redacted closing or lease evidence would be included only if there is an actual agreement and permission to publish it. Dates, corrections, and changes of plan should remain visible rather than be replaced with a polished success narrative.

## What a hash establishes

A hash of a precisely identified published file or record set lets a reader compare bytes against the recorded digest. To be useful, a future release needs the corresponding public files, the covered version or manifest, the hashing method, and publication evidence. A changed file needs a new dated version; an unexplained overwritten link is not a revision history.

Hashing cannot prove a deed is authentic, establish legal title, validate an underlying event, show that withheld material is complete, or turn a wallet into an ownership receipt. Putting a digest on-chain does not remove those limits. The promise here is a reporting plan, not an independently authenticated archive.

## Privacy before publication

Records require permission and redaction before publication. Private signatures, personal details, sensitive wire instructions, account information, precise GPS coordinates, and live locations stay out of the public set. County, variety, and soil details may be shared where safe and available.

Only the reviewed public record set should be hashed for public reporting; a digest is not a reason to expose private originals. Redactions limit what outsiders can establish from the record, and that limitation should be stated plainly. No photographs, closing documents, logs, or record digests are invented to fill an empty reporting calendar.

## Participation without entitlement

Possible off-chain polls could ask readers about a barn name, scarecrow, or onion nickname. They are advisory: the founder may disregard a result. They do not govern the LLC or give holders voting or management rights.

Voluntary gifts, a farm day, onion powder, roadside signs, or merchandise could be considered if actually offered and disclosed. Nothing is scheduled or guaranteed here. Holding $ONION does not reserve a harvest, gift, event place, or visit. LLC merchandise would be a separate purchase; proceeds belong to the LLC, not a holder distribution or investment return.

The story is readable without a wallet. The [farm arrangement](farm.md), [token interests](token.md), and independent [market observations](benchmark.md) remain distinct records.
